Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Last Updated: January 2026
We will refer to Attendance Tracker as "We" and you as "User". This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, name, profile picture, unique username, and academic major

Attendance Data: Subject names, attendance records, semester information, edit history with reasons

Usage Data: Login times, app interactions, and preference settings

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide attendance tracking functionality
  • Send attendance reminders and warnings
  • Maintain your academic records and edit audit trails
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Personalize your experience with themes and preferences

Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely in Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We implement industry-standard security measures
  • Edit history is maintained for data integrity and audit purposes

Admin Access

  • App administrators may access user data for technical support and maintenance purposes only
  • Admin access is logged and monitored
  • Data is never shared with third parties without consent

Your Rights

  • View, update, or delete your account data anytime
  • Export your attendance records to Excel format
  • Request account deletion (data removed within 30 days)
  • Opt-out of notifications
  • Edit past attendance records (within 7 days) with proper justification

Data Retention

Active accounts: Data retained indefinitely

Deleted accounts: Data permanently removed within 30 days (User can request immediate removal)

Backup data: Removed within 90 days

Edit audit logs: Retained for data integrity purposes

New Features & Data Usage

  • Edit History: When you edit past attendance, we store the original status, new status, edit reason, and timestamp for audit purposes
  • Milestone Tracking: We track attendance streaks and milestones for gamification features
  • Theme Preferences: We store your customization preferences locally and in your account

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
